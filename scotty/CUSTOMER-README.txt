Scotty Can Fix It Remote Support

This independent open-source RustDesk fork connects to remote.scottycanfixit.com.
Open the app only when you want support. Give your ID to your technician and
approve the incoming connection. You can reject or end a session at any time.
No shared password is included. Do not enable unattended access unless intended.

This release is unsigned. SignPath Foundation approval has not been granted.
